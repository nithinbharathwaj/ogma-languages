Ogma Spanish privacy policy

Effective date: 2 November 2026

Short version: Ogma Spanish does not collect, send, or share any data about you. It works offline and has no account.

What the app does not do

  • It has no internet access (the Android app does not request the internet permission).
  • It has no account or sign-in.
  • It has no ads, analytics, or crash-reporting tools.
  • It does not share anything with anyone.

What stays on your device

Your learning progress (lessons completed, scores, and your settings) is saved in a file named progress.json on your device. The course content and audio are built into the app.

Device backups

Your phone's own backup system may copy the progress file: Google's Android backup (to your Google account) or Apple's iCloud and device backups, if you have them turned on. This is done by Android or iOS, not by us. We never receive it and cannot access it. It lets you restore your progress on a new phone. Manage or turn off backups in your device settings.

Daily reminder

If you turn on the optional reminder, the app asks for notification permission and schedules a notification on your device at the time you choose. No server is involved. You can turn it off in the app or in your device settings.

Children

Ogma Spanish is intended for users aged 13 and older. Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If the policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.
